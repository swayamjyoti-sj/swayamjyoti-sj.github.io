INDUSTRY PARTNERSHIPS

Turn a materials question into a testable simulation study.

For R&D teams exploring deformation, disordered materials, finite element modelling or numerical optimization.

Proposed pilot: understand deformation before scaling up

Start with one material system, one loading condition and one reference dataset. Together, define whether atomistic or continuum modelling is appropriate for the engineering decision.

What your team brings

A defined engineering question, suitable material parameters or datasets, and a reference experiment or trusted simulation.

Potential deliverables

An agreed benchmark, documented modelling assumptions, reproducible input files, comparison against reference data and a short decision report.

How a pilot would work

  1. Scoping discussion: use case, data availability and expected decision.
  2. Written plan: scope, timeline, resources, budget, confidentiality and intellectual property.
  3. Benchmark study: compare model output against agreed evidence.
  4. Review: assess limitations and decide whether further work is useful.

Scope, pricing, availability and any institutional arrangements are agreed before work begins. No performance improvement is claimed before validation.
