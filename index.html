01 / ABOUT

Curiosity at

I study the behavior of materials across scales, combining physical modelling, numerical methods, and machine learning to understand complex systems.

My research spans metallic glasses, local structural excitations, molecular dynamics, finite element methods, and AI-driven materials discovery. I received my Doctor of Science from ETH Zurich and currently work as a visiting researcher at RWTH Aachen University.
