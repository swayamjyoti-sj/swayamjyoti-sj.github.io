Symbols and notation

Definitions, scope and interpretation of the mathematical symbols used across the proposal's 30 numbered equations.

The same letter can have different meanings in different equations. Each entry links to the equation(s) where it is used. Bold symbols are vectors or tensors; an overbar denotes a homogenized quantity; a dot denotes a time derivative; † marks an activation saddle/barrier; superscript p denotes plastic contribution.
