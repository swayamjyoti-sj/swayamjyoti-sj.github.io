Numerical Examples.

Concrete numerical demonstrations, each connected to a proposal statement. Verified numerical benchmarks are anchored to Atomistic Simulations to Study Metallic Glasses (ETH Zurich doctoral thesis, S. Swayamjyoti, 2016). Proposed algorithms use explicitly marked illustrative inputs.

Thesis / illustrative provenance
Scientific provenance. “Thesis parameters” means the numerical input is documented in the provided thesis or summarized in the proposal. “Illustrative calculation” means the numerical inputs are chosen to demonstrate the stated equation and are not new simulation results. The proposal includes future hypotheses and validations; this page does not claim they have already been established. It presently covers selected quantitative statements, not literally every sentence of the 18-page proposal.
