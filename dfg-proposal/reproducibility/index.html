DFG PROPOSAL · REPRODUCIBLE COMPUTATION · WP1–WP5

Reproducibility of every result

A full test plan for atomistic metallic-glass configurations, ARTn/NEB, AQS, activated kinetics, mesoscale plasticity, continuum FEM, material decohesion and the published research outputs.

Scientific status: These 35 explicit procedures are proposed tests—not completed or passed experimental results. The proposal calls for independent validation, data/code reuse and convergence; the page operationalizes those commitments. Exact numerical tolerances and sample sizes will be frozen after pilot convergence studies, not invented.

Three independent standards of evidence

Numerical verification

Does the implementation solve its stated equations? Verify analytic limits, convergence, conservation, Hessian stability, consistent tangent and energy/dissipation laws.

Computational reproducibility

Can a separate researcher recreate the exact run from pinned source, potential, seeds, dependencies, input manifests and job commands?

Scientific replication

Do conclusions remain statistically robust across independently prepared glasses and genuinely withheld loading histories without calibration leakage?

Statistical principle: repeat whole independent glass preparations, not just many correlated ARTn events, atomic displacements or frames from one sample. Preserve same-seed replay separately from new-seed replication. Treat numerical tolerances and stochastic confidence intervals differently.

TEST FAMILY 1

Inputs and preparation

R01

Environment rebuild

Recorded inputs: Pin source revision, compiler, libraries, container digest, potential checksum and run command.

Reproduction test: Clean reinstall and rerun reference tests.

Evidence / failure action: Build log, hashes; stop if dependencies cannot be reconstructed.

R02

LJ force and unit consistency

Recorded inputs: Record ε, σ, mass, cutoff, composition, periodic boundaries and mixing rules.

Reproduction test: Finite-difference force and energy derivatives; dimensional checks.

Evidence / failure action: Numerical tolerance report; fix discrepancies before production.

R03

Quench replay

Recorded inputs: Record parent structure, seeds, thermostat, cooling schedule and minimization thresholds.

Reproduction test: Same-seed rerun; separately generate independent quenched glasses.

Evidence / failure action: Energy, density, RDF, inherent-state distribution; report preparation sensitivity.

R04

Preparation convergence

Recorded inputs: Vary timestep, quench increment, minimizer and potential cutoff.

Reproduction test: Compare energy, pressure and structure against finer settings.

Evidence / failure action: Convergence plots and adopted setting.

R05

Finite-size ensembles

Recorded inputs: Prepare independently seeded samples at multiple cell sizes.

Reproduction test: Estimate means, variances and correlations versus size.

Evidence / failure action: Confidence intervals and finite-size limits.

TEST FAMILY 2

ARTn, NEB and AQS

R06

Saddle verification

Recorded inputs: Record stationarity and constrained Hessian spectrum.

Reproduction test: Verify exactly one unstable saddle mode, minimize both sides, refine sample paths by NEB.

Evidence / failure action: Gradient/Hessian residuals and endpoint paths; reject spurious saddles.

R07

Forward/reverse barriers

Recorded inputs: Record A, B, S energies and saddle IDs.

Reproduction test: Check ΔE(A→B)−ΔE(B→A)=U(B)−U(A).

Evidence / failure action: Per-event residual table; reject inconsistent connections.

R08

Uniqueness and low-tail convergence

Recorded inputs: Deduplicate geometry and energy; independently restart searches.

Reproduction test: Plot new unique-channel cumulative rate and escape-time quantiles versus search effort.

Evidence / failure action: Search-effort bands; incomplete catalogue requires bounded rates.

R09

Atomic descriptor parity

Recorded inputs: Archive nonaffine displacements, event moments and participation definitions.

Reproduction test: Recompute from original atomic states by independent analysis.

Evidence / failure action: Tensor/participation parity report; identify incorrect conventions.

R10

AQS stability refinement

Recorded inputs: Track lowest stable Hessian eigenvalue under load and energy minimization.

Reproduction test: Bisect critical strain brackets; distinguish onset from triggered cascade.

Evidence / failure action: Converged γc bounds and core locations.

R11

ARTn–AQS falsification

Recorded inputs: Freeze matching radius, loading direction and predictor before withheld comparison.

Reproduction test: Evaluate overlap and orientation against randomized-site and structure-only controls.

Evidence / failure action: Glass-level bootstrap CI; report correspondence failure if controls are not beaten.

TEST FAMILY 3

Thermal kinetics

R12

Arrhenius limits

Recorded inputs: Record each ΔG‡(T,σ,z), prefactor and approximation ΔG‡≈ΔE‡.

Reproduction test: Sweep ν and free-energy corrections; stress derivative tests.

Evidence / failure action: Sensitivity ranges, no unsupported mapping of LJ time to seconds.

R13

Event clock kernel

Recorded inputs: Use analytically known fixed-rate examples.

Reproduction test: Seeded Gillespie trials: channel frequencies, survival, mean and quantile checks.

Evidence / failure action: Monte-Carlo interval coverage; fix hazard sampling if inconsistent.

R14

Driven hazard integration

Recorded inputs: Define a piecewise rate schedule and strain ramp.

Reproduction test: Integrate accumulated hazard with time-step refinement.

Evidence / failure action: Convergence to known exact survival; avoid static-rate claim.

R15

Reversibility and detailed balance

Recorded inputs: Retain forward/reverse paths and equilibrium assumptions.

Reproduction test: Check stationary ratio where equilibrium applies; test driven cases separately.

Evidence / failure action: Diagnostic ratios and state distributions.

R16

Held-out waiting times

Recorded inputs: Freeze kinetic parameters on training glasses.

Reproduction test: Predict event identities and waiting-time quantiles on unseen preparations/temperatures.

Evidence / failure action: Calibration scores, uncertainty coverage; reject predictive rate transfer if outside bounds.

TEST FAMILY 4

Mesoscale stochastic elastoplasticity

R17

Plastic moment transfer

Recorded inputs: Store full event transformation tensor, volume and orientation.

Reproduction test: Compare integrated event moments to mesoscopic plastic strain; check work sign and units.

Evidence / failure action: Conservation residual and tensor tests.

R18

Stress redistribution

Recorded inputs: Specify spatial elastic kernel and boundary conditions.

Reproduction test: Compare inclusion response to analytical/finite-element reference; mesh and boundary sweeps.

Evidence / failure action: Kernel field residual and force balance.

R19

A/B/C ablation

Recorded inputs: A independent hazards; B elastic coupling; C state renewal.

Reproduction test: Common initial states, seeds, driving and calibration budgets.

Evidence / failure action: Matched stress-drop/avalanche comparison.

R20

Avalanche finite-size testing

Recorded inputs: Freeze event clustering, band-persistence and duration rules.

Reproduction test: Independent glass and spatial-size sweeps with glass-level bootstraps.

Evidence / failure action: Cutoff uncertainty, cross-size stability; no unsupported universal exponents.

R21

Held-out collective predictions

Recorded inputs: Calibrate interactions on training samples only.

Reproduction test: Predict independent relaxation, cascade probability, onset/location and history.

Evidence / failure action: Out-of-sample metrics versus no-coupling/null models.

TEST FAMILY 5

Continuum FEM and homogenization

R22

FEM software verification

Recorded inputs: Specify strain/stress conventions and element quadrature.

Reproduction test: Patch tests, finite-difference Jacobian/tangent checks, Newton and timestep refinement.

Evidence / failure action: Residuals, convergence rates, frame/objectivity checks.

R23

Hill–Mandel identity

Recorded inputs: Use P:F work-conjugate finite-strain RVE boundary conditions.

Reproduction test: Compare macro P:δF and averaged micro Pm:δFm across mesh and boundary conditions.

Evidence / failure action: Normalized work residual, separate energy and dissipation tests.

R24

RVE size and representativeness

Recorded inputs: Use independent RVE states, shapes, sizes, boundary types.

Reproduction test: Track mean AND variance of P, tangent and correlation lengths.

Evidence / failure action: Self-averaging plots; restrict local deterministic law if absent.

R25

Mesh-objective localization

Recorded inputs: Vary h, regularization/internal length and loading paths.

Reproduction test: Compare band onset, width, orientation and propagation.

Evidence / failure action: Mesh sensitivity and regularization limits.

R26

Withheld continuum prediction

Recorded inputs: Lock constitutive parameters and internal lengths.

Reproduction test: Predict independent multiaxial paths/temperatures/rates without refitting.

Evidence / failure action: Held-out stress and localization errors, model-form uncertainty.

TEST FAMILY 6

Conditional fracture and decohesion

R27

Separation calibration provenance

Recorded inputs: Record independent cohesive strength, Gc, mixed-mode and damage length evidence.

Reproduction test: Audit independent inputs versus plasticity calibration.

Evidence / failure action: Without separation evidence, mark fracture illustrative only.

R28

Traction–opening benchmark

Recorded inputs: Use known bilinear cohesive law and an energy-consistent damage update.

Reproduction test: Analytic versus numerical integration of traction and Gc, monotonic/unloading checks.

Evidence / failure action: Work/damage parity, irreversibility, nonnegative dissipation.

R29

Crack and mesh convergence

Recorded inputs: Prescribe crack geometry and boundary conditions.

Reproduction test: Refine interface/phase-field mesh, timestep and length parameter.

Evidence / failure action: Initiation threshold, crack path/arrest and energy balance convergence.

R30

Plasticity-only negative control

Recorded inputs: Run localized plasticity with no decohesion criterion.

Reproduction test: Ensure shear bands do not automatically become free cracks.

Evidence / failure action: False-positive crack diagnostics and separation-state audit.

TEST FAMILY 7

Statistics, HPC and release

R31

No-leakage partitions

Recorded inputs: Assign parent glass IDs and split at independent glass level.

Reproduction test: Audit all train/validation/test hashes and duplicate parents.

Evidence / failure action: Frozen split manifest; invalidate contaminated tests.

R32

Uncertainty accounting

Recorded inputs: Separate numerical, sampling, prefactor, model and experimental uncertainty.

Reproduction test: Bootstrap independent glasses, assess interval coverage and multiplicity.

Evidence / failure action: Confidence bands with stated sample counts.

R33

Cross-machine reproduction

Recorded inputs: Pin dependencies/MPI/BLAS and parallel algorithms.

Reproduction test: Rerun reference cases on independent machines; allow justified floating-point tolerances.

Evidence / failure action: Hardware/environment manifest and pass/fail CI logs.

R34

HPC budget audit

Recorded inputs: Log jobs, reserved cores, walltime, memory and output volume.

Reproduction test: Compute Σ allocated cores × elapsed hours and compare to declared budgets.

Evidence / failure action: Core-hour ledger; revise future requests from benchmarks.

R35

Independent result and figure recreation

Recorded inputs: Store input hashes, code tags, scripts, locked parameters and figure pipeline.

Reproduction test: External rerun of one full benchmark and regenerate all reported tables/plots.

Evidence / failure action: Audit report, unresolved failures and negative outcomes.

Release criterion: every claimed result must have traceable raw data, versioned code, frozen calibration/test split, numerical convergence and uncertainty evidence. On failed tests: correct the software, redo the run, widen uncertainty or narrow the scientific claim—never silently omit the failure.
