What the animation teaches

Rates and event moments pass from atomistic transition searches to interacting mesoscale events; stress work and statistical descriptors pass to constitutive models. Explicit atomic trajectories and every individual saddle are typically omitted, but their physical consequences cannot be ignored. The continuum approximation requires independent validation; fracture introduces separate damage and cohesion physics.
