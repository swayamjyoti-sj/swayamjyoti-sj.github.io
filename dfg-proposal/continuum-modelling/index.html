DFG PROPOSAL · WP4 / WP5

Continuum Modelling

A colour-coded mathematical reference for the five candidate constitutive regimes of metallic-glass plasticity, shear-band localization and the conditional fracture extension.

Research status: These equations are candidate formulations, not demonstrated phase boundaries. The relevant model is selected by independently tested accuracy, mechanical work consistency, statistical representativeness, mesh convergence and thermodynamic admissibility. Regimes may overlap.

01

Regime I — Deterministic local homogenization

Weak correlations and representative volumes

Strain decomposition

\[\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}=\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^e+\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p\]

Elastic stress

\[\boldsymbol{\sigma}=\mathbb C:(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}-\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p)\]

Mechanical equilibrium

\[\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}+\mathbf b=\mathbf0\]

Homogenized stress

\[\boldsymbol{\Sigma}=\frac1V\int_V\boldsymbol{\sigma}\,dV\]

Homogenized strain

\[\mathbf E=\frac1V\int_V\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}\,dV\]

Hill–Mandel work consistency

\[\boldsymbol{\Sigma}:\dot{\mathbf E}=\frac1V\int_V\boldsymbol{\sigma}:\dot{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}\,dV\]

Illustrative viscoplastic flow

\[\dot{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}^p=\dot\gamma_0\left\langle\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}-\sigma_y}{K}\right\rangle^n\mathbf N\]

Validity and limitations. Applicable when RVE statistics converge, fluctuations are weak for the observable, and local deterministic predictions pass independent tests.

02

Regime II — Stochastic plasticity

Near yielding and intermittent events

Thermally activated event rate

\[r_\alpha=\nu_\alpha\exp\left[-\frac{\Delta E_\alpha(\boldsymbol{\sigma},\mathbf q)}{k_BT}\right]\]

Plastic strain from discrete events

\[d\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p=\sum_{\alpha=1}^{N_c}\Delta\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p_\alpha\,dN_\alpha(t)\]

Mean event count

\[\mathbb E[dN_\alpha\mid\mathcal F_t]=r_\alpha(t)\,dt\]

Conditional mean plastic increment

\[\mathbb E[d\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p\mid\mathcal F_t]=\sum_\alpha r_\alpha\Delta\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p_\alpha\,dt\]

Conditional covariance

\[\operatorname{Cov}[d\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p\mid\mathcal F_t]=\sum_\alpha r_\alpha(\Delta\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p_\alpha\otimes\Delta\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p_\alpha)\,dt\]

No-event survival probability

\[P(N(t+\Delta t)-N(t)=0)=\exp\left[-\int_t^{t+\Delta t}R(s)\,ds\right]\]

Total hazard

\[R(t)=\sum_\alpha r_\alpha(t)\]

Validity and limitations. Mean/covariance formulas assume conditionally independent Poisson channels. Correlated avalanches need explicitly interacting event dynamics; the survival probability uses the no-event trajectory hazard.

03

Regime III — History-dependent plasticity

Interacting events, memory and structural renewal

Structural state variables

\[\mathbf q=(q_1,q_2,\ldots,q_m)\]

Constitutive response

\[\boldsymbol{\sigma}=\widehat{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon},\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p,\mathbf q,T)\]

Plastic-strain evolution

\[\dot{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}^p=\mathbf f(\boldsymbol{\sigma},\mathbf q,T)\]

State evolution

\[\dot{\mathbf q}=\mathbf g(\boldsymbol{\sigma},\mathbf q,T)\]

Example: deformation-induced change and relaxation

\[\dot\chi=A\dot\gamma^p(\chi_\infty-\chi)-\frac{\chi-\chi_{\mathrm{eq}}(T)}{\tau(T)}\]

Alternative linear hereditary representation

\[\boldsymbol{\sigma}(t)=\int_0^t\mathbb G(t-s):\dot{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}(s)\,ds\]

Isothermal dissipation inequality

\[\mathcal D=\boldsymbol{\sigma}:\dot{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}-\dot\psi\geq0\]

Validity and limitations. Internal-variable evolution and the linear hereditary equation are alternative or complementary descriptions, not a single compulsory constitutive law. Nonlinear coupling must satisfy thermodynamics.

04

Regime IV — Nonlocal plasticity and shear bands

Persistent localization and spatial correlations

Gradient-enriched free energy

\[\psi=\psi_e(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}-\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^p)+\psi_h(p)+\frac{H\ell_p^2}{2}|\nabla p|^2\]

Gradient microstress

\[\boldsymbol{\xi}=\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial\nabla p}=H\ell_p^2\nabla p\]

Energetic variational contribution

\[\frac{\delta\psi}{\delta p}=\frac{\partial\psi_h}{\partial p}-H\ell_p^2\nabla^2p\]

Dissipative flow

\[\dot p=\mathcal G(Y,T),\qquad Y\dot p\geq0\]

Alternative integral nonlocal average

\[\overline p(\mathbf x)=\frac{\int_\Omega K_\ell(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)p(\mathbf y)\,dV_{\mathbf y}}{\int_\Omega K_\ell(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)\,dV_{\mathbf y}}\]

Embedded-band displacement jump

\[\lbrack\!\lbrack\mathbf u\rbrack\!\rbrack=\mathbf u^+-\mathbf u^-\]

Embedded-band dissipated work

\[W_b^{\mathrm{diss}}=\int\mathbf t_b\cdot d\lbrack\!\lbrack\mathbf u\rbrack\!\rbrack-\Delta\psi_b^{\mathrm{stored}}\]

Plastic activity correlation

\[C_a(r)=\langle\delta a(\mathbf x)\delta a(\mathbf x+\mathbf r)\rangle\]

Illustrative exponential correlation decay

\[C_a(r)\sim e^{-r/\xi}\]

Validity and limitations. Gradient, integral nonlocal and embedded-band methods are alternatives for comparison. Complete microforce balances, boundary conditions, length-scale calibration and mesh-objectivity checks remain necessary.

05

Regime V — Phase-field damage and fracture

Conditional extension requiring independent fracture calibration

Damage bounds

\[0\leq d(\mathbf x,t)\leq1\]

Elastic degradation

\[g(d)=(1-d)^2+\eta\]

Regularized potential energy

\[\mathcal E=\int_\Omega\left[g(d)\psi_e^++\psi_e^-+\psi_p+\frac{G_c}{2\ell_d}d^2+\frac{G_c\ell_d}{2}|\nabla d|^2\right]dV-W_{\mathrm{ext}}\]

Illustrative stationary damage equation

\[-2(1-d)\psi_e^++G_c\left(\frac d{\ell_d}-\ell_d\nabla^2d\right)=0\]

Irreversible damage

\[\dot d\geq0\]

Natural boundary condition

\[\nabla d\cdot\mathbf n=0\]

Validity and limitations. Stationary damage equation applies where the irreversibility inequality is inactive. Calibrated toughness, crack initiation and thermodynamic plasticity–damage coupling are mandatory before claiming validated metallic-glass fracture predictions.

Common validity tests — WP4 & WP5

Quantitative criteria for selecting the simplest independently validated model.

Hill–Mandel work-consistency error

\[e_{\mathrm{HM}}=\frac{\left|\boldsymbol{\Sigma}:\dot{\mathbf E}-\langle\boldsymbol{\sigma}:\dot{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}\rangle_V\right|}{|\boldsymbol{\Sigma}:\dot{\mathbf E}|+W_{\mathrm{ref}}}\]

Statistical RVE convergence

\[e_{\mathrm{RVE}}(V)=\frac{|\overline Q(V)-\overline Q(V_{\mathrm{ref}})|}{|\overline Q(V_{\mathrm{ref}})|+Q_{\mathrm{scale}}}\]

Relative fluctuations

\[C_V(Q)=\frac{\operatorname{Std}(Q)}{|\mathbb E[Q]|}\]

Independent predictive error

\[E_m=\sqrt{\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\left(\frac{Q_{m,i}^{\mathrm{pred}}-Q_i^{\mathrm{ref}}}{s_i}\right)^2}\]

Simplest valid model

\[m^*=\underset{m\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{valid}}}{\operatorname{argmin}}\,\operatorname{Complexity}(m)\]

Predeclared consistency thresholds

\[E_m\leq\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pred}},\quad e_{\mathrm{HM}}\leq\varepsilon_{\mathrm{HM}},\quad e_{\mathrm{mesh}}\leq\varepsilon_{\mathrm{mesh}},\quad\mathcal D_m\geq0\]

Interpretation. Mechanical work consistency does not imply statistical RVE convergence. Use held-out states, independent conditions, distribution-level checks for stochastic predictions and independently calibrated fracture observables. Define numerical tolerances before testing.
