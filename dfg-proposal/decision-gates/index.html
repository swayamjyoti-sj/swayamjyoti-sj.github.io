Research Decision Gates

A color-coded flowchart of every major go / revise / restrict decision across the 36-month atomistic → kinetics → mesoscale → continuum → conditional fracture research programme.

Scientific distinction: Gates A–D are the proposal's formal decision logic; the M6 pilot and M36 delivery checkpoints are included to show the entire programme. All outcomes below are conditional and not claims of completed research.

Pilot benchmark checkpoint

Decision

Are reference glass preparations and initial event searches reproducible?

YES · advance

Continue broader searches and matched AQS benchmarks.

NO · adapt

Narrow preparation / cell-size matrix; fix minimization, quench and saddle verification.

Information transfer and evidence

Pass: Reference states, preparation metadata, initial path schema

Do not pass: Do not interpret early event counts as complete kinetics

Operational checkpoint (not a formally numbered proposal gate)

WP1 → WP2 A · M12

Catalogue adequacy

Decision

Have rate-relevant low-barrier channels and escape-time uncertainties stabilized?

YES · advance

Transfer the rate-weighted and bounded event catalogue into kinetics.

NO · adapt

Add independent saddle searches or propagate incomplete-catalogue rate bounds.

Information transfer and evidence

Pass: Unique transitions, reverse paths, energy barriers, event tensors, uncertainties

Do not pass: Discovery frequency must not become physical transition probability

Proposal Gate A

↓
WP2 → WP3 B · M20

Thermal–athermal transfer

Decision

Can the matched ARTn and AQS tests predict held-out events better than controls?

YES · advance

Use validated susceptibility signals in coupled mesoscale predictions.

NO · adapt

Keep distinct thermal and athermal selection rules; report failed correspondence.

Information transfer and evidence

Pass: Held-out spatial overlap, orientation, barrier-stress and first-passage evidence

Do not pass: Do not assume thermally activated channels are AQS instability sites

Proposal Gate B

↓
WP3 → WP4 C · M27

Collective-response transfer

Decision

Do interacting event models generalize, and is a work-consistent continuum closure plausible?

YES · advance

Test converged RVEs, algorithmic tangents and continuum localization.

NO · adapt

Quantify non-self-averaging; retain stochastic, nonlocal or memory-enriched response.

Information transfer and evidence

Pass: Event-driven RVE stress histories, dissipation, correlations, uncertainty

Do not pass: Do not fit a local deterministic law where response does not self-average

Proposal Gate C

↓
WP4–WP5 D · M33

Conditional fracture decision

Decision

Are independent separation properties and adequate numerical resources available?

YES · advance

Calibrate damage and test crack initiation, growth/arrest, energy and mesh convergence.

NO · adapt

Keep the validated plasticity/localization deliverable; omit predictive crack claims.

Information transfer and evidence

Pass: Independently calibrated traction–separation or fracture energy plus validated continuum fields

Do not pass: Shear bands and LSE events alone do not determine material decohesion

Proposal Gate D

↓
WP5 · Final deliverables M36

Final acceptance and dissemination

Decision

Have held-out tests, reproducibility artifacts and uncertainty limits been reported?

YES · advance

Release documented code, results, validation matrix and domain-of-validity map.

NO · adapt

Document limitations, negative results and unvalidated extensions explicitly.

Information transfer and evidence

Pass: Reproducible workflow, held-out metrics and failure boundaries

Do not pass: Do not label proposed simulations as completed validated results

Final programme milestone

Scientifically valid final outcomes

Predictive chain

Verified, transferable atomistic kinetics and collective plasticity, with a validated continuum regime and possibly separately validated decohesion.

Restricted closure

Reliable kinetic and mesoscale prediction but deterministic continuum or crack prediction restricted by self-averaging and separation-data limitations.

Falsified link

Rigorous quantitative evidence that ARTn–AQS transfer, rate preservation or continuum closure fails in an identified regime. Negative results remain scientifically valuable.
